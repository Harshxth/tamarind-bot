# Tamarind Table: Restaurant Knowledge Base

Tamarind Table is a Hyderabadi (South Indian) restaurant in the Seminole Heights neighborhood of Tampa, Florida. It is known for Hyderabadi dum biryani, which is sealed in a pot and slow cooked for 3 hours, curries made with spice blends toasted and ground every morning, and breads baked in a tandoor. The dining room has 40 seats. Phone: (813) 555-0142.

## Menu

Spice scale: None, Mild, Medium, Hot. Curries and biryani can be made milder or hotter on request; just tell your server or say so when ordering by phone. Starters and breads cannot be adjusted.

### Starters
- **Samosa Chaat**, $9. Crushed potato samosa, chickpea curry, tamarind and mint chutneys, sev. Spice: Mild. Vegetarian. Contains gluten.
- **Gobi Manchurian**, $11. Crispy cauliflower tossed in a garlic, chili and soy glaze. Spice: Medium. Vegan. Contains gluten (soy sauce and batter).
- **Chicken 65**, $13. Hyderabadi fried chicken with curry leaves and red chili. Spice: Hot (the spiciest dish on the menu). Gluten-free. Halal.
- **Paneer Tikka**, $14. Tandoor charred cottage cheese, peppers and onion in a yogurt marinade. Spice: Mild. Vegetarian, gluten-free.

### Mains
- **Butter Chicken**, $19. Smoked tandoori chicken in a silky tomato and butter sauce. Spice: Mild. Gluten-free. Halal. Contains cashew and dairy. Most popular curry.
- **Lamb Rogan Josh**, $24. Slow braised lamb shoulder with Kashmiri chili, fennel and ginger. Spice: Medium. Gluten-free. Contains cashew.
- **Chana Masala**, $15. Chickpeas simmered with onion, tomato and toasted spices. Spice: Mild. Vegan, gluten-free.
- **Goan Fish Curry**, $22. Gulf grouper in a coconut, kokum and tamarind curry. Spice: Medium. Gluten-free, dairy-free. Contains fish.
- **Palak Paneer**, $16. Spinach puree, cottage cheese, garlic tadka. Spice: None. Vegetarian, gluten-free.

Mains are served with steamed basmati rice. Breads are ordered separately.

### Biryani
- **Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani**, $20. Basmati rice and marinated bone-in chicken sealed and slow cooked for 3 hours, served with raita and mirchi ka salan. Spice: Medium (can be made Mild or Hot). Gluten-free. Halal. Best-selling dish.
- **Goat Biryani**, $24. Bone-in goat, saffron, fried onion, mint. Spice: Medium. Gluten-free. Halal.
- **Vegetable Biryani**, $17. Seasonal vegetables, whole spices, rose water. Spice: Mild. Vegan, gluten-free. Served with raita (ask for it on the side to keep it vegan).

### Breads
- **Garlic Naan**, $4. Tandoor baked with garlic butter and cilantro. Vegetarian. Contains gluten and dairy.
- **Laccha Paratha**, $5. Flaky layered whole wheat flatbread. Vegetarian. Contains gluten.

### Desserts
- **Gulab Jamun**, $7. Warm milk dumplings in cardamom syrup. Vegetarian. Contains gluten and dairy.
- **Mango Kulfi**, $8. Slow reduced milk ice cream with Alphonso mango. Vegetarian, gluten-free. SEASONAL: currently NOT available. It returns in mango season (spring).

### Drinks
- **Mango Lassi**, $6. Yogurt, mango, a pinch of cardamom. Vegetarian, gluten-free.
- **Masala Chai**, $4. Assam tea brewed with ginger, cardamom and clove. Vegetarian, gluten-free.

Tamarind Table does not serve alcohol and does not allow bring-your-own alcohol.

## Diet quick reference

- **Vegan dishes:** Gobi Manchurian, Chana Masala, Vegetable Biryani.
- **Vegetarian dishes (includes vegan):** Samosa Chaat, Gobi Manchurian, Paneer Tikka, Chana Masala, Palak Paneer, Vegetable Biryani, Garlic Naan, Laccha Paratha, Gulab Jamun, Mango Kulfi, Mango Lassi, Masala Chai.
- **Gluten-free dishes:** Chicken 65, Paneer Tikka, Butter Chicken, Lamb Rogan Josh, Chana Masala, Goan Fish Curry, Palak Paneer, all three biryanis, Mango Kulfi, Mango Lassi, Masala Chai. All breads, Samosa Chaat, Gobi Manchurian and Gulab Jamun contain gluten.
- **Halal:** All chicken and goat served is halal. Lamb is not certified halal.
- **Nuts:** Butter Chicken and Lamb Rogan Josh sauces contain cashew. No other dish contains nuts as an ingredient.
- **Allergy note:** Everything is cooked in one shared kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, dairy and fish, so cross-contact is possible. Guests with severe allergies should call (813) 555-0142 before visiting.

## Reservations and seating

- Walk-ins are welcome for parties of 1 to 5.
- Parties of 6 or more should call ahead at (813) 555-0142. The largest table seats 12.
- Online booking is not available; reservations are by phone only.
- Friday and Saturday evenings from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM are the busiest times; expect a 20 to 30 minute wait without a reservation.

## Takeout, delivery and catering

- **Takeout:** Order by phone for pickup. Most orders are ready in 20 to 25 minutes; biryani orders over 4 portions need 45 minutes.
- **Delivery:** Available within 5 miles through the major food delivery apps.
- **Catering:** Biryani trays (chicken, goat or vegetable). Half tray $65, serves 8 to 10. Full tray $120, serves 18 to 20. Goat biryani trays add $15. Order with at least 48 hours' notice by phone. Raita and salan included.

## Deals and extras

- **Lunch special:** Tuesday to Friday, 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM. Any biryani with a Mango Lassi or Masala Chai for $15.
- **Kids' plate:** For children 10 and under, $10. Choice of Butter Chicken or Chana Masala, with rice and a small naan.
- **Gift cards:** Sold in the restaurant in any amount from $25.
- **Payment:** All major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.
