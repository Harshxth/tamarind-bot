Cooked the long way

We toast and grind our spice blends every morning. Biryani goes into a sealed pot over low heat for three hours, the way it is done in Hyderabad, so the rice steams in the juices of the meat.

Forty seats, one kitchen, and a menu marked for diet and heat on every line, so first-timers and regulars both know what they are ordering.

Good to know

Do you take reservations?

Walk-ins are welcome. For parties of 6 or more, call ahead at (813) 555-0142 and we will hold a table.

Takeout and delivery?

Call in a pickup order, or order delivery within 5 miles through the major delivery apps.

Allergies and nuts

Butter Chicken and Lamb Rogan Josh sauces contain cashew. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so for a severe allergy please call before you order.

Is the meat halal?

Yes. All chicken and goat is halal.

Bringing kids?

The kids' plate (Butter Chicken or Chana Masala with rice and naan) is $10 for ages 10 and under.

About Saffron

Saffron is a Botpress AI chatbot that answers guest questions for Tamarind Table, so staff can stay with the guests in the room during the dinner rush.

What it knows

One knowledge-base document: all 18 dishes with prices, spice and diet flags, plus hours, reservations, catering, deals and parking.

How it behaves

Warm and brief. Talks only about Tamarind Table, never invents a dish or price, and sends allergy and booking questions to the phone line.

How it was tested

Diet, spice, hours, catering, sold-out and off-topic questions were checked in the Botpress emulator before publishing.

Where it runs

Botpress Webchat is embedded in this page. The site is plain HTML and CSS on GitHub Pages.
