Tamarind Table, Tampa

Meet Saffron, your dining assistant.

Saffron is an AI chatbot for Tamarind Table, a Hyderabadi kitchen in Seminole Heights. It answers guest questions about the menu, prices, diets, spice levels, hours, catering and house policies, using the restaurant's own knowledge base, any time of day.

See what it knows

Why this bot exists

The same five questions, every night.

Is the biryani spicy? Is it gluten-free? Are you open Monday? Can I book for eight? Do you cater? A small restaurant answers these by phone during the dinner rush. Saffron answers them instantly, from one source of truth, so staff can stay with the guests in the room.

How it works

Built with Botpress

  1. Knowledge base. The menu, hours and house policies were written up as one document and uploaded to the bot's Botpress knowledge base, so every answer is grounded in the restaurant's real data instead of the model's guesses.
  2. Instructions. The bot has a short persona: warm, brief, answers only about Tamarind Table, never makes up a price or a dish, and sends guests to the phone line for reservations and allergy questions it cannot confirm.
  3. Tested in the Studio emulator. Questions about diets, spice, hours, catering and unavailable items were checked against the knowledge base before publishing.
  4. Published and embedded. Botpress Webchat is loaded with two script tags at the bottom of this page. The page itself is plain HTML and CSS built in VS Code and hosted on GitHub Pages.
